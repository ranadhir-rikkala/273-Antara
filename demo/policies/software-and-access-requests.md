# Access to GitHub, Jira and Confluence

This policy applies to everyone, including contractors.

To get access to GitHub, Jira or Confluence, ask the helpdesk.

Employees get access to GitHub, Jira and Confluence the same day they ask.

Contractors need approval from their sponsoring manager, and their GitHub, Jira and Confluence access ends on their contract end date.

Access to private GitHub repositories is granted per team. Ask your team lead to add you to the right GitHub team.
