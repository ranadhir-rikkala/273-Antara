# Incident Severity Levels and On-Call

This policy applies to everyone, including contractors.

SEV1: a customer-facing outage or a confirmed security breach. The on-call engineer must respond within 5 minutes, and status updates go out every 30 minutes.

SEV2: a major feature is degraded for many customers. Response within 15 minutes, updates every hour.

SEV3: a minor issue with a workaround. Response within one business day.

To page the on-call engineer, open an incident in the helpdesk and choose the affected system. The helpdesk pages the right on-call rotation automatically.

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident needs a written blameless review within 5 business days.
