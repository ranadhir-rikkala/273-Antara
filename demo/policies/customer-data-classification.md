# Customer Data Classification and Handling (Confidential)

This document is confidential to employees. Contractors receive only the summary in their onboarding training.

Customer names, email addresses, phone numbers and order history are confidential data. Store them only in approved company systems, such as the CRM and the orders database. Never keep them in spreadsheets, personal cloud storage or personal devices.

Payment card tokens, government ID numbers and health information are restricted data. Store them only in the restricted data cluster, where every read is logged.

Architecture documents, runbooks and internal metrics are internal data. Share them with employees only, never outside the company.

Customer data is deleted 90 days after a customer closes their account, unless a legal hold applies.

Exporting more than 1,000 customer records at once needs approval from the data protection officer.
