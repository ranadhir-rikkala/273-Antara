# Password and Authentication Policy

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the previous 10 passwords.

Passwords for standard accounts must be rotated every 180 days. Passwords for administrator accounts must be rotated every 90 days.

To reset a forgotten password, use the self-service reset portal and verify with MFA. If MFA is unavailable, the IT helpdesk can reset the password after verifying identity with a manager.
