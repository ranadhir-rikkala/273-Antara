# Reporting Phishing and Suspicious Email

This policy applies to everyone, including contractors.

If an email looks suspicious, do not click its links or open its attachments. Use the Report Phishing button in the mail client, which sends it to the security team and removes it from your inbox.

If you already clicked a link or opened an attachment, report it to the helpdesk immediately as a security incident and disconnect from the network until IT calls you back.

The security team never asks for credentials or one-time codes by email, chat or phone.

Phishing tests are sent every quarter. Anyone who fails two tests in a row repeats the security awareness training.
